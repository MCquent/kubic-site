Extensions

Small tools, right inside Kubic

Extensions open in Kubic’s Extensions tab. They use the textures of the game installed on your PC, work offline, take on the colours of your theme — and keep what you were doing when you switch tabs.

  1. 1In Kubic, open the Extensions tab: this catalogue is listed there.
  2. 2Click Install. Kubic shows what the extension may do, then checks the file’s fingerprint.
  3. 3Or download the .kubicext file below and drop it on the Extensions tab.

The catalogue

Free, and updated from here: as soon as a new version is published, Kubic offers the update in its Extensions tab.

Each extension runs in its own sealed frame: no network, no access to your files or to your Minecraft account. It only gets what its card lists, and Kubic asks you again before changing your skin. Game textures are never shipped with an extension: they are read from your own game.
